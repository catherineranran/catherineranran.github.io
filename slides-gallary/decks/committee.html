Speaker Name · Trust in algorithmic advice

Two findings that don’t fit together

AVERSION

People abandon algorithms after seeing them err

After watching a forecasting algorithm make mistakes, people chose it less often, even when it still outperformed a human forecaster.

Dietvorst, Simmons & Massey (2015)

APPRECIATION

People prefer algorithmic to human advice

In estimation and forecasting tasks, people weighted advice more when they thought it came from an algorithm than from a person.

Logg, Minson & Moore (2019)

Our predictions

H1

Advice that explains its reasoning is followed more often.

H2

A confidence score adds to the effect of the reasons.

H3

The effect holds after people see the algorithm err.
