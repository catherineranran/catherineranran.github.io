Web page or slide file?

Present from a web page when

  • you want to send one link before or after the talk;
  • the talk has motion, video or a live figure;
  • you will present from your own laptop;
  • text should stay sharp on any projector, since nothing is a picture.

Bring PowerPoint or Keynote when

  • the venue wants your slides uploaded in advance;
  • you rely on speaker notes and a timer in presenter view;
  • co-authors will edit the deck with you;
  • you present from a shared computer at the venue.

Before any talk

  • Download the .html and open it once with the network off. On a Mac the decks use built-in fonts; other computers fetch web fonts the first time.
  • Keep a PDF backup: open the .html in Chrome or Safari and print to PDF. Each slide prints as one 16:9 page.
  • Try your clicker. The decks treat Page Down and Page Up as next and back, which is what most clickers send.
  • To jump during questions, add #slide-8 to the address, or press Home and End.

A vibecoding by Ranran Li · ranranli.net

The sample talk is invented, and every slide with numbers says “sample data”. The four references are real papers. The PowerPoint and Keynote files use fonts that come with macOS; the web presentations fall back to Google Fonts on other computers.
